# Customer Response Desk — guided demo
Public demo: https://adiai.si/customer-response-desk.html
Hosted guide: https://adiai.si/customer-response-guide.html
Suggested time: 3–5 minutes. All sample content is fictional.

1. From Today, choose Explore the sample to open Maya's delivery enquiry. Note the case ID, owner, next action and deadline.
2. Choose Record complaint. The category changes; the case ID and earlier history stay together.
3. Under Customer reply, choose Use reply template, edit and Save draft. This records a draft without sending a message.
4. Under Update ownership & next action, choose Waiting for team. Retain the owner, enter a next action, a future Next due and a Waiting reason. Select Save changes.
5. Under Escalate or resolve, enter a fictional Resolution outcome, confirm completion for this scenario and select Resolve case. Today can show it when Include resolved cases is enabled.
6. Under Reopen this case, enter a reason, owner, next action and future due date. Choose Reopen with next action. Go to Team handover, filter by owner and download the handover workbook.

The workbook contains Cases, Handover and History sheets. Handover includes only matching open cases. The demo resets on reload; private-pilot saving needs separately approved accounts.

For two-person scenarios and a feedback template, use customer-response-trial-kit.md.
